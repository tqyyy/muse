# Job-board recruiter replies

## Set the reply rules before opening messages

- Take the reply authorization, the background facts and the hard limits from the current task, and write them down before you open any conversation.

- Treat a background paragraph the user gave you as judgment material for your replies, not as text to forward to a recruiter.

- Decide from the task whether applying or replying comes first, and pause the lower priority work when the task says to.

- Keep the limits that apply to every reply, such as no invented experience, no alternate contact details and no fee agreement, unless the task explicitly authorizes otherwise.

## Read each conversation before you reply

- Open the recruiter conversation and read the recruiter's actual question before you compose anything.

- Resolve which posting and which recruiter the message belongs to, and do not reply from a preview line alone.

- Check what the conversation already asked and answered, so you do not repeat a question or send the same resume twice.

## Compose a short reply to the question asked

- Answer only what the recruiter asked, in a short natural tone that fits the conversation.

- Draw the facts you need from the background material and phrase them for that one question, instead of pasting the whole paragraph.

- Send the online resume or profile when the recruiter asks for it and the task authorizes sending it.

- Respond positively to an interview invitation within the scope the task authorizes, and confirm the time and place the recruiter stated.

- Decline or leave unanswered a message the task does not authorize you to accept, and record why you left it.

## Send once and verify in the conversation

- Send each reply once through `browser.browse` on the job board, and confirm the reply appears in the conversation before you move on.

- Report sent, declined and still pending replies separately, with the recruiter and posting for each one.

- Name any reply that may have gone beyond the task limits at wrap up instead of hiding it in the total.

- If nothing here fits, look on the web for a maintained API or MCP server and set it up with [Custom Connectors and MCPs](/opt/muse/skills/custom-connector-setup/SKILL.md), or build a custom connector.

## Websites

- [Boss直聘](https://www.zhipin.com/) for recruiter messages, posting details and the contacted list. Carry the task criteria, the background facts and the reply limits into `browser.browse`, and verify the conversation, the reply you sent and the resume action there.

## Alternatives for better results

- Draft replies for the user to approve one at a time when the task does not clearly authorize autonomous replies.

- Batch the replies at a checkpoint after applying when the task prioritizes application throughput, so neither queue blocks the other.
